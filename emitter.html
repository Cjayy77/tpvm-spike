Capture run

  1. Press S to save reference.png. That is your ground truth.
  2. F for fullscreen, Tab to hide the panel. Lights off, no other screens in frame.
  3. Manual camera app. Lock ISO and white balance. Shoot video at each exposure: 1/30, 1/60, 1/120, 1/250, 1/1000. Ten seconds each.
  4. Press M and shoot one more at 1/60. That is your control.
  5. Look at the screen yourself. Sweep your eyes side to side across it — smearing trails mean the phantom array is showing.
  6. Run attack.py on each clip against reference.png.

Reading it

Browsers cannot guarantee frame-accurate present. This rig tells you whether banding appears on your hardware at all — it does not tell you how a native D3D12 flip-model build would behave. Treat a good result as permission to build the native version, not as the result.
